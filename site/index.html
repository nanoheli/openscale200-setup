    Set up your OpenScale 200

    This assistant prepares the electronics of your helicopter in four steps. It takes about five minutes.

    1. ⚡
      Power upConnect the flight battery and USB.
    2. ⚙
      Configure the ESCsMain motor (1) and tail motor (2) get their AM32 settings.
    3. ⬆
      Update RotorflightInstall Rotorflight (master ).
    4. ✓
      Apply the settingsLoad the tested OpenScale 200 configuration.

    You will need

    • A computer with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge
    • A USB data cable for the flight controller
    • A charged flight battery
    • The main and tail rotor blades removed
    This browser can't talk to the helicopter. Open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a desktop computer. Firefox, Safari and mobile browsers don't support USB access.

    Step 1 of 4

    Power the helicopter with the battery

    The ESCs only respond when the flight battery is connected. USB power alone isn't enough.

    Safety first: remove the main and tail rotor blades. Motors can twitch while the ESCs are being programmed.
    1. 1
      Remove the rotor bladesMain and tail blades off, helicopter on a stable surface.
    2. 2
      Connect USBPlug the USB cable into the flight controller and into this computer.
    3. 3
      Connect the flight batteryPlug in the battery. You should hear the ESC start-up tones.

    Step 2 of 4

    Configure the ESCs

    Both AM32 ESCs are programmed through the flight controller. Keep the battery connected.

    The firmware on this flight controller is too old to program ESCs. Update the firmware and apply the settings first. The assistant brings you back here afterwards.

    Step 3 of 4

    Update Rotorflight

    Installs Rotorflight from master for the GOOSKY F4 Mini flight controller.

    InstalledConnect to check
    →
    New
    The flight controller is completely erased. All settings are restored in the next step. The flight controller restarts in bootloader mode and briefly disappears from the computer.
    PrepareBootloaderEraseWriteVerifyRestart

    Select the flight controller in bootloader mode

    Your browser will list a device called STM32 BOOTLOADER (or "DFU in FS Mode"). Select it and click Connect.

    The device doesn't show up
    • Windows: the bootloader needs the WinUSB driver. Install it once with Zadig (select "STM32 BOOTLOADER", driver "WinUSB") or the ImpulseRC Driver Fixer, then click the button again.
    • Linux: add a udev rule for 0483:df11 so your user can access it.
    • Still nothing? Unplug USB, hold the BOOT button on the flight controller, plug USB back in, release the button.

    Apply the helicopter settings

    Loads the tested configuration : receiver, swashplate, servos, governor, rates and PID tuning.
